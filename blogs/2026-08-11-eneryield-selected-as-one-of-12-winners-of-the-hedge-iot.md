Eneryield has been selected as one of 12 winners of the HEDGE-IoT Open Call 1, receiving EU funding to further develop and validate our AI Fault Prediction and Analytics solution for electric power systems. Through HEDGE-IoT, Eneryield will continue advancing its technology while contributing to the digitalisation and electrification of Europe’s energy sector.
